An example of the process

“Our projects keep missing their deadlines.”

Start with what happened

Three of our last four projects were delivered late. What else did each person observe?

Everyone can contribute their perspective without losing the thread.

Keep the conversation in sync

Think wide. Then think narrow.

Good ideas can get lost when one person is exploring and another is already judging. Solve Guide helps people make room for both kinds of thinking.

Make room for possibilities

Think wide

Explore what could be causing the problem without having to defend or dismiss every idea as it appears.

Find a direction together

Think narrow

Consider the evidence behind each explanation and work toward a decision the people involved can support.

Local-first collaboration

You keep your own copy.

Your work lives on your device, so you can keep thinking when you’re offline. When you reconnect, your changes come together with your collaborators’ contributions.

Private by design

Share on your terms

Your personal notes stay yours. Shared Issues are encrypted before they leave your device, so the infrastructure carrying them cannot read their contents.

A record you can return to

Understand how you got here

See the reasoning behind a decision and what happened when you tested it. Changing your mind doesn’t erase what you previously agreed on.

Bring your own agent

Give your agent a place at the table.

Use the agent you already work with to help investigate an Issue or challenge an explanation. Its contributions join the same conversation you’re having with the people involved.

The value is in reaching understanding together. An agent can help you get there; agreement still belongs to the people who have to live with the decision.

Connecting through WebMCP

WebMCP gives compatible browser agents a structured way to work with your open Solve Guide session. You choose which agent to use and authorize its access. Contributions follow Solve Guide’s rules and retain their provenance.

Information you share with an external agent is also subject to that provider’s data practices.

Ask your agent

“What evidence would help us distinguish between these explanations?”

Bring the answer back to the Issue so everyone can examine it.

Why Solve Guide exists

A good discussion should lead somewhere.

You don’t need everyone to start with the same explanation. You need a way to understand each other’s reasoning and find a change worth trying. Solve Guide keeps that work connected to the original problem, so you can tell whether it made a difference.
